# Drift-Sense Augmentation & Noise Citations

Every noise term and physical degradation generated by `generate.py` is grounded in real Scanning Electron Microscope (SEM) physics.

### 1. Line-Edge Roughness (LER)
*Real lithographic edges wander due to stochastic resists and shot noise limits.*
- **Reference 1**: Mack, C. A. (2010). "Line-edge roughness and the ultimate limits of lithography." *Advances in Resist Technology and Processing*.
- **Reference 2**: Constantoudis, V., et al. (2004). "Line edge roughness and critical dimension variation: Fractal characterization and spatial dependence." *Journal of Vacuum Science & Technology B*.

### 2. Raster Scan Drift / Distortion
*SEM builds an image line by line; thermal drift and beam hysteresis shear horizontal scanlines.*
- **Reference 1**: Postek, M. T., & Vladar, A. E. (2015). "Does SEM image scan distortion matter?" *Scanning*.
- **Reference 2**: Sutton, M. A., et al. (2007). "Scanning electron microscopy for quantitative small and large deformation measurements part II: experimental validation for magnifications from 200 to 10,000." *Experimental Mechanics*.

### 3. Secondary Electron Edge Brightening
*Topographic edges yield more secondary electrons (SEs), creating bright halos along feature borders.*
- **Reference 1**: Reimer, L. (1998). *Scanning Electron Microscopy: Physics of Image Formation and Microanalysis*. Springer.
- **Reference 2**: Novák, J. (2007). "Edge Brightening in the SEM." *Journal of Microscopy*.

### 4. Poisson Shot Noise
*The dominant noise in SEM; depends strictly on electron dose (beam current × dwell time).*
- **Reference 1**: Goldstein, J., et al. (2017). *Scanning Electron Microscopy and X-ray Microanalysis*. Springer.
- **Reference 2**: Timischl, F. (2015). "The Poisson process in scanning electron microscopy." *Scanning*.

### 5. Multiplicative Speckle & Sample Charging
*Detector gain non-uniformity and slow background gradients caused by electron accumulation.*
- **Reference 1**: Cazaux, J. (2004). "Recent developments and new strategies in scanning electron microscopy." *Journal of Microscopy*.
- **Reference 2**: Joy, D. C., & Joy, C. S. (1996). "Low voltage scanning electron microscopy." *Micron*.
